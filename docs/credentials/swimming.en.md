Alongside animation and programming, I have taken part in swimming and lifesaving events. This page preserves two participation certificates from 1403 and keeps their printed facts separate from my personal recollection of a first-place finish.

## My competition recollection

I recall placing first in a swimming competition at Rasad Pool, Maragheh. I do not remember the event date, stroke, distance or age category. Neither photographed certificate states a placing, so this recollection is not presented as a rank certified by these documents.

The certificate names Athar Pool, while my recollection names Rasad Pool. They are kept distinct here rather than being silently treated as the same event. The documents support participation; the first-place claim comes from my own recollection.

> Keyboard shortcuts work at my desk; in the pool, I still have to do the whole lap. 🏊⌨️😄

## Lifesaving event: Nedaye Omid Festival

The upper document records participation in the first edition of the Nedaye Omid Festival lifesaving competitions in 1403. It addresses Dear Athlete without an individual name. The handwritten county field is not confidently legible; unclear signature details and the small decorative quotation are not reconstructed. This is a participation record, not a professional lifeguard licence.

## Swimming festival participation

The lower document names Bardiya Alizadeh and a swimming festival for Sacred Defence Week at Athar Pool (اطهر), Maragheh. Its handwritten date is 403/6/25; the body supplies the year 1403, so the expanded reading is 1403/06/25. The certificate-number field is blank. No placing, stroke, distance or age category is printed.
